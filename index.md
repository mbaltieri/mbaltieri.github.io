  <p class="section-kicker">Agent foundations</p>
  <h2 id="home-intro-title">What makes a system an agent?</h2>
  <p class="home-intro__lead">Manuel develops mathematical theories for understanding agency: how agents are distinguished from their environments, what they model and how they pursue goals, and how these ideas can support safer AI.</p>
  <p>His research at Araya and as an ARIA R&amp;D Creator connects category theory, control, probability, dynamical systems, cognitive science, and artificial life.</p>
