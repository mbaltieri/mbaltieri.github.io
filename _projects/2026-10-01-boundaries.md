# Boundaries

Our work on [agents and agency]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link _projects/2022-06-18-theories-of-agency.md %}) uses categorical systems theory, where an agent and its environment interact through a fixed interface. When boundaries change, interfaces change with them, and it becomes unclear how an agent's identity is preserved. In [The Emperor's New Markov Blankets]({{ site.baseurl }}{% link news/_posts/2021-01-09-friston-blankets.md %}), we examined how Markov blankets came to be used to define the boundary between an agent and its environment.

Boundaries are also a direction for AI safety research. Manuel attended the [Conceptual Boundaries Workshop](https://formalizingboundaries.ai) in Austin (February 2024), and was one of the research organisers of the Mathematical Boundaries Workshop in Berkeley (April 2024), which aimed to build mathematical models of boundaries ([retrospective](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/NitP3AsMeuycJQbfa/retrospective-on-mathematical-boundaries-workshop)).
