## Paper accepted at ICML 2026

Manuel's work with Yivan Zhang and Ziyan Luo was accepted at the [International Conference on Machine Learning 2026](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2026). The paper develops a compositional framework for identifying behavioral structures preserved by state abstraction in reinforcement learning. The [poster and conference page](https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/poster/62002), [conference paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/zhang26dy.html), and [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25357) are available online.
