## The Stream of Computation

Manuel's work with Ryota Kanai and Yuwei Sun was published in the [Journal of Consciousness Studies](https://www.ingentaconnect.com/content/imp/jcs/2026/00000033/f0020007/art00003). The paper considers temporal continuity as a missing ingredient in current artificial systems and proposes a roadmap for persistent, recursive computation. A [preprint](https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/c6jnd_v1) is also available.
