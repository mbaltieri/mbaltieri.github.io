## World models: environment, agent and joint agent-environment models

A new [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20401), with Filippo Torresan, Yivan Zhang, Alexander Boyd, and Fernando E. Rosas, distinguishes world models by the channel they predict: the environment, the agent, or their joint interaction.
