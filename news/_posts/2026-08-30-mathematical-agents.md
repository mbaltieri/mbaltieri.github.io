## Mathematical approaches to the study of agents

Manuel's work with Keisuke Suzuki is forthcoming in *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B*. The article reviews mathematical approaches to agency through individuality, normativity or goal-directedness, and interactional asymmetry. The [preprint](https://osf.io/rqu7s_v2) is available online.
