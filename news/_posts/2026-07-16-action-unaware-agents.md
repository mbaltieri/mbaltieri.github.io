## Active inference for action-unaware agents

Manuel's work with Filippo Torresan, Keisuke Suzuki, and Ryota Kanai is now [published in Neurocomputing](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2026.134399), volume 700, article 134399. The paper compares active inference agents that know their past actions with agents that must infer them, using two navigation tasks. A [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12027) is also available.
