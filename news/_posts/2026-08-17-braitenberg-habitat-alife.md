## A Braitenberg vehicle's habitat

Manuel's work with Fernando Rosas and Filippo Torresan appeared at the [Artificial Life Conference 2026](https://2026.alife.org/). The paper uses Braitenberg vehicles to examine how an agent's environment can be defined through the perceptions and actions available to it. The paper is available [here](/assets/pdf/BraitenbergHabitat.pdf).
